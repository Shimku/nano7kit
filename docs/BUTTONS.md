# Buttons

Sleep/Wake is the button on top. Home is the round button under the screen.
Volume Up and Volume Down are on the left side. `./install` shows these steps
at the moment you need them.

## Recovery (DFU) mode

Used once, to write the firmware.

1. Hold Sleep/Wake (top) and Home together.
2. The nano turns off, the Apple logo appears, then the screen goes black.
3. Keep holding about one more second (long enough to say "nano").
4. Let go of both buttons. The screen stays black: that is recovery (DFU) mode.

If Finder offers to download recovery software, click Cancel.

## Disk Mode

Used to copy apps and games.

1. Hold Sleep/Wake (top) and Home together.
2. As soon as the Apple logo appears, quickly switch to holding Volume Up and Volume Down.
3. Keep holding them until the screen says "Do not disconnect".

## Restart

1. Hold Sleep/Wake (top) and Home together until the Apple logo appears.
2. Let go and wait for the Home screen.
