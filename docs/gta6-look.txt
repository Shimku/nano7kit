GTA 6 NanoApp look notes
========================

Fan-made parody for a 240x432 iPod nano. Recreate the public GTA VI look in
original code-drawn pixels. Do not ship logos, fonts, trailer frames, key art,
screenshots or music from the publisher.

Logo
----
Small "GRAND THEFT AUTO" stacked tight above a large Roman VI, condensed bold
bitmap. VI is two tall numerals (V then I), filled with a vertical sunset
gradient (blue-violet, magenta, pink, orange). Cream inner edge, thin dark
outline. The I is a flat-serifed bar. Palms sit beside the letters, not inside
them. No third-party typeface.

Trailer intro
-------------
Fade up from black. Short scenes, each with a slow pan or fake zoom:
1. Sunset over water, palms in silhouette, pastel towers on the far shore.
2. Night street, wet asphalt, MOTEL/BAR/KEY neon, parked cars, reflections.
3. A car on a causeway over teal water at dusk.
4. Two people silhouetted (Nia Voss, Wade Pike) against the sun.
Then the VI logo reveal. Tap at any point jumps to the menu.

Loading menu
------------
Diagonal panel collage in the numbered-entry cover style: character portraits,
a beach, neon, a car, a gator, city towers. Original Mangrove Key cast, not
copied poses. "TAP TO START" on a dark bar.

In-game (GBA Advance grain, VI colour)
--------------------------------------
Warm sunlit roads with kerbs and dashes, pastel art-deco roofs (one colour
per block, parapet, AC, vents), palms with drop shadows on sidewalks, parked
cars, beach foam. Neon Wharf and south of the canal go dark blue/purple with
lit windows and glowing edges. HUD: circular radar, green money, gold stars.
Stick and buttons are translucent so the street shows through.

Composition
-----------
Portrait 240x432. Big shapes, high contrast, few colours per tile. Keep
touch hitboxes. Home name stays "GTA 6".
